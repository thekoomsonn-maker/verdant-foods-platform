Logging a harvest
Five steps, under two minutes, with or without signal.
1

Crop

Pick from pictures: tomato, garden egg, yam, maize

2

How much

Big number pad, then kg, crate or bunch

3

When and where

Harvest date and which plot

4

Photo and methods

One photo; tick the farming methods used

5

Price and save

Set price, tap save

Right after saving

VF-TOM-00042

The trace number appears at once, even offline, from a reserve of numbers kept on the phone.

What this covers in Verdant's document

Batch Inventory & Tracking: every harvest is a batch with a permanent trace number. Photos and farming methods are added to the farm's record. The photos are always real, taken by the farmer.

Savings groups (VSLA)
The group's paper ledger, now safe on a phone.

Members, group rules, weekly savings, loans and the cash box, recorded in one pass at the meeting, even with no signal.

Overview

Group savings, members, loans out, cash in the box, next meeting

Meeting

Record each member's shares and repayments in one go

Members

Add members, see each person's balance

Loans

Amount, interest rate, schedule, what's been repaid

Ledger

Every transaction in order. Nothing can be quietly changed.

Group rules

Share price, loan interest rate, cycle length

Phase 1 starts with 20 savings groups

Savings and payouts move by MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AT Money. Balances are shown large and clear, with the time they were last updated.

Comes later

Credit scores, crop insurance and small investments, from Phase 3.

Working offline
No signal is normal. The app never waits for it.
1

Save on the phone

Every entry is stored on the phone first

2

Show it's waiting

"2 entries saved, will send when online"

3

Send when signal returns

Works on 2G, 3G or 4G, in the background

4

Confirm

"All sent", with the time

SituationWhat the app does
Logging a harvest offlineSaves it and gives the final trace number straight away
Recording a savings meeting offlineSaves the whole meeting; totals are locked once the group agrees
The same record changed twiceThe newest change wins; the VSLA admin is told if a meeting record clashes
Money figuresAlways show when they were last updated, so nobody trusts an old balance

Verdant's document asks for this offline-first mode. The backend developer builds the receiving side of the sync.

Testing and release
Three ways for Verdant to try the app.
1

Browser preview

A link on Vercel to click through the screens on any phone or laptop. Best for design and flow reviews.

2

Test installs

Testers install from an email invite (Firebase App Distribution, free) and get every update. The real test of offline use.

3

Play Store

Internal testing, then public release, through Verdant's own Google Play account.

Test on a low-cost phoneA phone like the ones farmers use, to check speed and size
Test with weak signalSwitch data off and on during harvest logging and meetings
Test in every launch languageNative speakers check the wording and voice prompts

20 savings groups

Phase 3: finance

  • Carbon rewards for farmers

  • Small investments
