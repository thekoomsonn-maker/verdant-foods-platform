01 · Executive product summaryFoundations
01Executive product summary
The whole journey of food, in one transparent system.

Verdant is not an online grocery store. It is one ecosystem, with several role-specific experiences, that connects food demand, regenerative supply and farmer finance.

PrincipleConnect demand, supply and farmer finance without making the platform feel fragmented. One design system, one data spine, different jobs per role.
The loop the product must make visible
Grow→Harvest→List→Buy→Deliver
→Trace→Pay & save→Grow again
Product outcomeWhat the product must doSource
Premium market accessConsumer marketplace plus a dedicated B2B procurement experience.A
Farmer enablementSimple, offline-capable tools for farm profile, harvest batches, inventory, orders and payouts.A
TraceabilityPermanent batch IDs from day one; order-linked QR journey page in Phase 2.A
VSLA digitalisationA cloud ledger for members, group rules, contributions, loans and cash position.A
Operational controlOne admin workspace for verification, orders, deliveries, payments and oversight.B

02 · Business & product contextFoundations
02Business & product context
Three problems, two pillars, one bottom line
Problem · Planet
Soil is being depleted

Conventional farming degrades topsoil and drives greenhouse-gas emissions.

Problem · Trust
Food origin is hidden

Buyers want transparency; legacy supply chains conceal origin and footprint.

Problem · People
Farmers are excluded

No structured savings, reliable credit, insurance or digital payments.

Pillar 1
AgTech Marketplace & Traceability Matrix
  • Direct-to-buyer engine: procurement, contracts, logistics
  • Every batch logged seed → delivery: origin, methods, carbon, harvest window
  • QR on packaging opens the journey
Pillar 2
FinTech Space
  • Digital VSLAs: secure cloud ledger replacing paper
  • Micro-investment & climate-indexed insurance P3
  • MoMo, card and bank payments with instant producer settlement
Demand side: premium buyersSupply side: producers & VSLAs
High-end restaurants & culinary brandsSmallholder regenerative growers
Supermarkets & organic establishmentsCommercial sustainable agri-businesses
Eco-conscious individual consumersDigitised savings groups (VSLAs)
Commercial malls & institutional buyersLocal farming cooperatives
Triple bottom line
Profit · People · Planet

Success is measured on all three. Public impact numbers show verified data only.
