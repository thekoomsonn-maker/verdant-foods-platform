What we take, and what we turn into Verdant's own
Reference principleBecomes in Verdant
Giant type × cut-out produceHomepage and campaign heroes; produce overlaps headlines.
Grocery palette blocksLime, orange, plum, sun, blush as merchandising grounds.
Fintech big numeralsVSLA balances and farmer payouts: large figures, small decimals.
Warm calm dashboardB2B, farmer and admin density: stone canvas, white panels, one highlight.
Festival patternsSeasonal campaign stripes and pattern bands.
Agritech in the fieldTechnology shown at work in Ghanaian fields, art-directed and never stock.
Signature devices
Trace Frame

Hairline frame + mono ID tab around the hero produce. Makes traceability visible.

Verdant Crate

The recurring campaign object, with produce rising out of a stamped crate.

Produce silhouettes

Flat, faceless, slightly irregular shapes for tiles, empty states and patterns.

Headline formula

"Grown by ___. ___ by you." Repeatable, and it carries provenance.
