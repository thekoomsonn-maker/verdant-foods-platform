# Verdant Foods — Product Brief

Sources of truth: *Verdant Foods Integrated Corporate Strategy & PRS v1.0 (June 2026)* for business logic; the NEXKOOMSON master brief (Oct 2026) for design direction.

## The product in one line
**The whole journey of food — from the people who grow it to the people who buy it — in one transparent digital ecosystem.**
Grow → Harvest → List → Buy → Move → Deliver → Trace → Pay → Grow again.

## What Verdant is
A Green AgTech / FinTech company with two integrated pillars (PRS §3):
1. **AgTech Marketplace + Traceability Matrix** — farm-to-table commerce for premium businesses and individual consumers; every batch logged seed → delivery, viewable via QR.
2. **FinTech space** — Digital VSLAs (cloud ledger replacing paper), micro-investment & climate-indexed insurance (Phase 3), unified payments with MoMo payouts.

## Who it serves
| Demand side | Supply side |
|---|---|
| High-end restaurants & culinary brands | Smallholder regenerative growers |
| Supermarkets & organic stores | Commercial sustainable agri-businesses |
| Eco-conscious individual consumers | Digitised savings groups (VSLAs) |
| Malls & institutional buyers | Local farming cooperatives |
Plus **Verdant operations** (admin) and **Extension Officers** (onboarding, field).

## It must feel like
A modern African AgTech commerce ecosystem: fresh, credible, transparent. Not a generic farm site, NGO site, grocery store, or fintech dashboard with vegetables added.

## Visual balance
60% clean / premium / functional · 25% fresh / agricultural / organic · 15% expressive / editorial.
Consumer marketplace = most expressive. B2B → Farmer → Admin → VSLA become progressively more systematic, always visibly Verdant.

## Success measures (Triple Bottom Line)
Profit (fiscal viability) · People (rural asset creation, financial equity) · Planet (soil restoration, emissions). Impact metrics on public pages show **verified data only**.

## Constraints that shape design
- Farmer & VSLA use: low-end Android, 2G/3G, offline-first (NFR-1.1, 1.2) → large controls, few steps, plain language, visible sync state, cards not tables on mobile.
- Financial-grade trust (NFR-2) → calm, precise, auditable UI for ledgers and payments.
- Do not invent features beyond the PRS. Items in the master brief not found in the PRS are marked *proposed* in PRODUCT-ARCHITECTURE.md.

## Working process
1. Planning (done) → 2. Figma Design Lab → 3. Figma test screens (website: Homepage, Marketplace, Admin dashboard; app: Farm and Savings spaces) → review → all screens and prototype → website build (Next.js, Vercel) and app build (Flutter) → handover.
Design is done **Figma-first**; code implements approved Figma.
