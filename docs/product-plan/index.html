Verdant Foods · Product plan
Verdant in one page
The whole journey of food in one place you can trust.

Verdant connects the people who grow food, the people who buy it, and the savings groups that keep farmers going. It is one connected system, not just an online shop.

Better markets

A shop for households and a dedicated buying tool for restaurants, hotels and supermarkets.

Simple tools for farmers

A phone app to record farms and harvests, sell stock, handle orders and get paid. It works without signal.

Food you can trace

Every harvest gets a permanent number. Buyers scan a code to see where their food came from.

Savings groups on a phone

The paper ledger of village savings groups, made safe and clear.

Verdant Foods · Product plan
The problem and the plan
Three problems, two answers.
01

Soil is wearing out

Conventional farming drains topsoil and adds to climate change.

02

Buyers can't see where food comes from

Supply chains hide the origin and footprint of what we eat.

03

Farmers are left out of finance

No easy savings, credit, insurance or digital payments.

The marketplace

  • Farmers sell straight to households and businesses
  • Every harvest is recorded from seed to delivery
  • A QR code on the order shows the full story

The money side

  • Village savings groups move from paper to phone
  • Fast payouts to farmers by Mobile Money
  • Later: crop insurance and small investments

Who uses Verdant
Eight kinds of user, each with a different need

Visitor

Browses the site and shop without signing in. Can check out as a guest.

Shopper

Buys quickly and tracks orders. Account tools stay light.

Business buyer

Restaurants, hotels and shops. Needs stock, bids, deliveries and invoices.

Farmer

Savings member

Sees their own savings, shares and loans.

Savings admin

Records meetings, members and group rules for the whole group.

Extension officer

Signs up farmers and savings groups in the field. Sends farms for checking.

Verdant team

Checks farms, runs orders, deliveries and payments, and oversees everything.

Our recommendations
How we keep Verdant simple to use
QuestionOur answer
Are the menus too long?No. Team tools use 5 groups, business tools 7 items, and the farmer app 4 tabs plus a "Log harvest" button.
Is the farmer app simple enough?Yes. One main action, works offline, local languages and voice prompts.
Do business tools have the right focus?Yes. Built for speed, with bids as the official plan describes.
Is the shopper account too busy?No. Orders first, "Buy again", and no charts.
Are the team tools well organised?Yes. Grouped by Sales, People, Trust, Money and Settings, with a queue for farm checks.
Is the savings group scope right?Yes. Group rules added. Credit, insurance and investment wait for Phase 3.
Is tracing pitched right for Phase 1?Yes. Record every harvest in Phase 1; the public QR page follows in Phase 2.

Roadmap
Build the base, then trust, then finance.
Months 1 to 6
Months 7 to 18
Month 19 onward

Phase 1: pilot

20 savings groups and 5 restaurant partners

  • Website, shop and checkout
  • Shopper account
  • Business tools with bids and repeat deliveries
  • Farmer app with harvest records
  • Savings groups
  • Verdant team tools and farm checks
  • Farm and harvest date on every product

Phase 2: tracing and delivery

The full QR tracing system and cold-chain partners

  • QR code on every order
  • Public trace page
  • Collection and delivery tracking
  • Cold-chain delivery partners
  • Soil and carbon filters fully live
  • Better reports

Phase 3: finance

Money tools for farmers

  • Weather-based crop insurance
  • Rewards for carbon stored in soil
  • Credit scores from savings and sales
  • Small investments
  • Invoice financing for businesses

Later features stay hidden until they are ready. The harvest records kept from day one are what make Phases 2 and 3 possible.
