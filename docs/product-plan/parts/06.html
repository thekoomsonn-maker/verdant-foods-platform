Phase 1: pilot

20 savings groups and 5 restaurant partners

  • Website, shop and checkout
  • Shopper account
  • Business tools with bids and repeat deliveries
  • Farmer app with harvest records
  • Savings groups
  • Verdant team tools and farm checks
  • Farm and harvest date on every product

Phase 2: tracing and delivery

The full QR tracing system and cold-chain partners

  • QR code on every order
  • Public trace page
  • Collection and delivery tracking
  • Cold-chain delivery partners
  • Soil and carbon filters fully live
  • Better reports

Phase 3: finance

Money tools for farmers

  • Weather-based crop insurance
  • Rewards for carbon stored in soil
  • Credit scores from savings and sales
  • Small investments
  • Invoice financing for businesses

Later features stay hidden until they are ready. The harvest records kept from day one are what make Phases 2 and 3 possible.

Decisions made
Ten questions, now settled
Farmer appOne Android-first app, works offline, under 15MB, with a savings group space inside
Stock-up packsYes, as ready-made bundles. No shopper subscriptions in Phase 1.
Guest checkoutYes. Name, phone and address; updates by SMS
Extension officersYes, from Phase 1
BidsFarmer accepts, declines or counters once. 48 hours to reply.
QR codesOne per order. Business deliveries also get one per crate.
Trace numbersVF-TOM-00042. Made by the backend, with spare numbers kept on the phone.
PaymentsMTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, Visa, Mastercard, Ghanaian banks
PhotographyArt-directed photography and video for marketing; real farmer photos on records
LanguagesEnglish, Twi, Dagbani, Dagaare at launch; Gonja, Waale, Ewe, Ga in the pilot year
