What changed in this version

We checked the first version against Verdant's official business plan line by line and corrected what didn't match.

Everything else from version 1 stays: the seven experiences, business tools that work like real software, a simple farmer app and a savings ledger people can trust.

BeforeNow, and why
Farmer and savings tools were websitesOne mobile app that works offline. The official plan asks for this.
Subscriptions for shoppersSubscriptions in the plan are repeat deliveries for restaurants and hotels, so they now sit with business buyers.
Businesses sent "quote requests"Businesses place bids, and can filter harvests by soil health and carbon data, as the plan describes.
Savings groups had no rulesAdded group rules: share price and loan interest rate.
QR codes per harvest onlyOne QR code per order, showing every harvest inside it.
Public trace page in Phase 1Moved to Phase 2, where the plan places it. Phase 1 still records every harvest.
One "group lead" roleSplit into savings group member and savings group admin. Extension officers added.
Payments barely coveredMoMo, cards and bank payments, plus farmer payouts, are now fully covered.
Impact numbers on the homepageOnly verified figures are shown. Nothing made up.
