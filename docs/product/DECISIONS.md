# Decisions made — 13 October 2026

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Farmer app | One Android-first Flutter app, offline, under 15MB, with a savings-group space inside |
| Stock-up packs | Yes, as ready-made bundles. No shopper subscriptions in Phase 1 |
| Guest checkout | Yes — name, phone, address; updates by SMS; account offered after payment |
| Extension officers | Yes, a Phase 1 role: field sign-up of farmers and savings groups, farm checks queue |
| Bids | Buyer bids on a listed harvest; farmer accepts, declines or counters once; Verdant team can step in; 48-hour expiry |
| QR codes | One per order (label + receipt). Business deliveries also get one per crate |
| Trace numbers | VF-TOM-00042 format. Backend owns numbering; app keeps a reserve so offline farmers get final numbers |
| Payments | MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money, Visa, Mastercard, Ghanaian bank transfer. Payouts to MoMo or bank |
| Photography | Art-directed photography and video for marketing; real farmer photos on farm and harvest records |
| Languages | Website: English and French, plus Twi (Asante), Akuapem Twi, Fante, Ga and Ewe; Dagbani and Gonja to follow with native-speaker review. App: voice prompts on key steps |
