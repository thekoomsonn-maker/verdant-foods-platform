# Verdant Foods — Reference Guide

The reference board (69 screens, `Reference/` + `reference 1/`) is an **approved research board, not optional inspiration**. This guide records *why* each cluster works and *what Verdant takes from it*. Nothing is copied literally.

Approved selection (13 Oct 2026): Gromuse palette + type×produce, Wise numerals, Kresho dashboard, field agritech, Gromuse stories, festival patterns, dashboard shots.

Priority when references conflict: **Verdant logo & identity → clarity → usability → brand → reference aesthetic.**

---

## A. Verdant's own material (#1, #2, logo)
- Logo green sampled at **#024639** — a teal-leaning deep green, not a pure green. Leaf **#5F9F0D**.
- The previous site (#1) used aerial crop-row photography under a dark scrim: keep the *architectural aerial* idea, lose the thin-light type and dot carousel.
- **Take:** the colours, exactly. Round monoline wordmark stays untouched; we contrast it with a chunky grotesk rather than imitate it.

## B. Target "Make a ___ run" (#5–#15)
Why it works: one sayable headline formula repeated endlessly; one branded hero object (the basket); products in flight; flat saturated grounds that rotate per message; big price callouts; dense coupon grids that still feel ordered.
- **Take:** a repeatable *formula* → "Grown by ___. ___ by you." A recurring *object* → the Verdant Crate. Flat produce-tint grounds for campaign modules. Large superscript prices.
- **Leave:** red basket, Target type, coupon clutter.

## C. Gromuse grocery identity (#16–#24, #27–#29)
Why it works: giant tight grotesk interacting with cut-out produce (#21, #24); confident price typography; cut-out product imagery on tinted wells.
- **Take:** type × produce overlap in hero/campaign moments; cut-out produce on tinted wells in product cards; superscript decimals.
- **Take (approved 13 Oct 2026):** the palette logic — deep green + slime/fresh lime + blaze orange + plum on cream — adopted as Verdant's accent set.
- **Leave:** skewed card walls, glossy phone mockups, cart-with-carrot logo idea. Differentiate from Gromuse through Bricolage type, the Trace Frame, produce silhouettes and the crate device.

## D. Granja Urbana + Naná (#25, #26, #36–#40)
Why it works: flat, slightly imperfect produce silhouettes in a tight earthy palette on cream; illustration carries joy without faces.
- **Take:** the **illustration language** — produce silhouettes, cream ground, tomato/maize/aubergine/olive harmony (sits beautifully beside #024639). Patterns for packaging and campaign bands.
- **Leave:** the Brazilian street-poster distress textures and stamp type.

## E. LaVespa / Diego (#3, #4) and Farm Day (#41) — **rejected for product UI**
Faced characters and kawaii vegetables read as children's brands; incompatible with B2B procurement and VSLA finance credibility. Their only lesson — saturated flat colour blocks — is already covered by D.

## F. Kresho, SAEROK, Genlab (#31, #42–#48)
Why it works: warm stone canvas, deep olive/green, one pale highlight; data shown sparingly; photography is tactile (hands in soil, crates).
- **Take:**
  - **#44 dashboard** is the admin/farmer density model: warm off-white canvas, white panels with hairline borders, one dark bar highlighting "now", small highlight tooltip, quiet sidebar.
  - **#42/#43 framed-subject device** → becomes the **Trace Frame**, Verdant's signature.
  - **#47 SAEROK box** → packaging/crate photography direction.
  - **#48 growth bubbles** → impact metrics can be shown as scaled forms, not just numbers.
- **Take also:** the pale highlight (→ Verdant Fresh Lime) for one-at-a-time emphasis in charts.

## G. Wise (#33–#35)
Why it works: enormous, tight, bold numerals with small decimals; heavy condensed caps lines; warm, real people photography.
- **Take:** balance/payout typography for VSLA and farmer payments (`metric-l`, superscript decimals). People photography direction: real, warm, mid-action.
- **Leave:** Wise lime, Wise Sans.

## H. Pinterest agritech boards (#49–#69) — **anti-reference for style**
Agrovia, Agroton, Tanigro, Harvesta etc. are the generic agritech genre: wheat-field heroes, glass pills, lime CTAs on dark green, everything in floating rounded cards.
- **Take only:** layout density and stats arrangement (e.g. #54 stat row, #56 field dashboard information layering).
- **Avoid:** wheat (not a Ghanaian crop context), glass pills. Agritech-in-the-field imagery (#30) is approved as a direction, but art-directed in Ghanaian fields, never stock.

## I. Yandex Market festival (#32)
Picnic stripes and patterned grounds — potential seasonal campaign pattern, low priority.

## Photography direction (synthesised)
1. **Produce as hero** — cut-out or tight macro on tinted grounds; Ghanaian produce first: tomatoes, garden eggs, plantain, yam, kontomire, okra, scotch bonnet, pawpaw, maize.
2. **Land as architecture** — aerial/low-angle fields, rows, soil texture, early light; Eastern & Volta region landscapes.
3. **Hands at work** — harvesting, sorting, weighing, crating; faces optional; never posed-with-basket.
4. **Movement** — collection points, crates on trucks, cold store, delivery hand-off.
Art-directed (directed camera, lighting, composition), internationally competitive, believable African environments.
